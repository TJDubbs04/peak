# Changelog

Every change people using Peak would notice, newest first. Peak's versions are `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`
([semantic versioning](https://semver.org)): a patch fixes things, a minor version adds or changes something, a major
version is a big change in what Peak is. Downloads: [releases](https://github.com/TJDubbs04/peak/releases).

## [1.0.0] — coming soon

Peak's first public release. Free to use.

- **The notch, alive:** the song playing (Music, Spotify, your browser or any app in macOS's Now Playing, with its
  cover or a video's thumbnail), a timer, a download, a call or a meeting about to start beside the notch; hover to
  open Peak, click or pin to keep it open.
- **Home:** the song with its controls and a bar to move through it, two players at once, today's calendar with Join,
  calls with Mute, downloads, timers, keeping the Mac awake, screenshots and downloads that just arrived, and the Timer,
  Keep Awake and Audio quick actions.
- **Files:** hold files, folders, links and text by dropping them on the notch, drag them out as copies, choose several,
  send them with AirDrop.
- **Moments:** the volume, a charger, headphones, a download or timer done, a new screenshot, a meeting starting, a call
  ended.
- **Window snapping:** drag a window toward the notch for six layouts and Snap Assist, or use ⌃⌥ shortcuts.
- **Settings:** Open at login, what Peak shows, Files and AirDrop, window snapping, Peak beside the notch, privacy notes,
  and About.
